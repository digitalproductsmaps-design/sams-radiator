Our Story

Built on Family Tradition

From apprentice to master craftsman — Sam's journey in metalwork.

Learning from the Best

In 2017, Sam walked into his uncle's workshop with nothing but curiosity and a willingness to learn. Under his uncle's patient guidance, he mastered the art of radiator repair, metal fabrication, and precision welding.

What started as an apprenticeship quickly became a lifelong passion. Sam learned that every repair tells a story — whether it's a farmer's truck that keeps the harvest moving, or a family's car that carries them safely home.

"My uncle taught me that good work isn't about speed — it's about doing it right the first time. That lesson stays with me every single day."

Today, Sam runs his own shop, bringing that same dedication to every project. From complex radiator rebuilds to custom metal creations, he treats every job as if it were his own.

What We Do

Our Services

Comprehensive radiator and metalwork solutions for vehicles, farms, and homes.

Radiator Repair

Complete radiator repair including leak detection, core replacement, and pressure testing. All vehicle types from daily drivers to heavy-duty trucks.

Metal Tank Repair

Expert repair of metal tanks including top tank repairs. Professional welding and soldering on aluminum, brass, and copper for lasting results.

Chicken Feeders & Drinkers

Custom-built durable chicken feeders and drinkers made from quality metal sheeting. Designed for farms and poultry operations of any size.

Chimneys

Custom metal chimney fabrication and repair. Built to withstand high temperatures and harsh weather with precision metalwork.

Metal Sheet Works

Precision metal sheet work for custom projects, brackets, panels, and specialized components. If you can dream it, we can build it.

Custom Fabrication

Specialized custom metal fabrication for any need. From automotive parts to agricultural equipment, we bring your ideas to life.
